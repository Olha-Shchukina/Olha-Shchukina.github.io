I'm Olha Shchukina

Wedding videographer and photographer in Madrid

Trained in Film Directing at the Kyiv National University of Theatre, Cinema and Television, I began my creative journey through visual language, careful composition and the influence of cinematography. During my university years I started producing videos for small brands and their social media, developing my eye for visual storytelling, a foundation I later completed with a professional photography course in Madrid.

2024 was a turning point for me: almost at the same time as I got married, I was asked to film my first wedding. It was one of the most rewarding experiences of my life, revealing the deep beauty of capturing human relationships and showing me the value of preserving real emotions.

All that emotion and learning go straight into how I live every event. On the wedding day I don't stand in a corner: my way of working is active and close. I bring energy, liven up the atmosphere, suggest creative ideas and guide you with momentum, but I also know exactly when to step back to capture spontaneous photos and video, letting the most authentic moments flow 100% naturally.

Here you will find a selection of my work. I hope you enjoy it as much as I enjoyed creating it, because, after all, life is too short for a boring wedding.
